## Baseline Summary Review of the Distiller Model (before any changes)

**Model:** `tkSDISW/Distiller`, branch `master`, commit `3898091` (2026-04-20), `Distiller Project.aird`
**Method:** read-only. I browsed each layer's main element types and ran `verify_model` on OA, SA, LA and PA. **No changes were made.**
**Purpose of this entry:** a baseline to compare against before the model is edited.

**What the model is about:** a water distiller. OA covers *Provide Safe Drinking Water*, including monitoring, maintenance, install, certify and deploy. SA/LA/PA then carry the same core flow: *Distill Water* decomposes into Heat Water, Boil Water, Condense Steam and Drain Residue. Actor-side functions cover water, heat, storage, distribution and discharge. The core flow is refined into logical components (Evaporator, Condenser, Drain, Diverter Assembly) and then into physical ones (Mechanical Boiler, Condenser, Drain Valve, Diverter Assembly with Splitter and Feed Valve). The PA also has physical links for steam, drainage, backup and main pipelines.

### Observations worth a human look
These came from reading names and structure. Some may be intentional.

1. **Spelling in names.** Each of these appears in the model as written:
   - "Deploymeny Personnel" (OA entity)
   - "Maintain and Serivce System" (activity or function at OA, SA, LA and PA)
   - "Water USer" (actor at SA, LA and PA)
   - "Maintainance" (SA functional exchange)
   - "Dirt Water Feed" (PA component exchange)
2. **Placeholder SA actors.** *A 7* and *A 8* are default-named actors at SA.
3. **PA structure looks like it holds two overlapping decompositions.** Elements seem to be duplicated at two levels:
   - *Condenser* appears under *Physical System* and under *Water Distiller*.
   - *Diverter Assembly* appears twice (under *Physical System* and under *Water Distiller*), each with its own *Splitter* and *Feed Valve*. A third *Splitter* sits directly under *Physical System*.
   - *Evaporator* and *Drain* sit beside *Mechanical Boiler* and *Drain Valve*.
   - This may be work in progress or an alternative design. It is worth confirming which is current before editing the PA.
4. **No requirements in the model.** Requirement browsing at SA and requirement-package browsing at OA both returned nothing. One SA diagram is titled "with requirements".
5. **No interfaces at LA.** Component exchanges exist, but the LA interface list is empty.
6. **Duplicate-looking names.**
   - SA has two functional exchanges named *Safe Drinking Water*.
   - LA has two component exchanges named *Hot Water*.
   - These may be legitimate parallel flows, but they are indistinguishable by name.
7. **Naming conventions vary.** Examples are "Test Chain" vs "Test chain", and the LA chain named *Distill Water* sits under the function of the same name.
8. **Traceability across layers not checked.** I did not examine realization links between OA, SA, LA and PA. The capability counts also differ by layer (OA 6, SA 3), so it is unclear how far OA capabilities are carried down.

### Not covered in this review
OA entity exchanges and processes, SA component exchanges, the data model (classes, exchange items, data types), realization and allocation links, and diagram contents. Other details were not examined element by element.

### Suggested next steps
- Decide whether the PA duplication (item 3) is intentional.
- Agree whether to fix the spelling items (item 1) and remove the placeholder actors (item 2).
- Review requirements and traceability if they are needed for this work.
